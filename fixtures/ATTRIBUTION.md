# Fixture provenance

All residents, observations, street names, agency records, coordinates-as-jurisdiction, and portal tickets in the local demonstration are fictional. Coordinates are used for spatial testing and do not assert a real municipality's boundary or ownership.

`images/curb-before.png`, `images/curb-after.png`, and `images/pothole.png` are original synthetic illustrations generated for Neighborhood Fixer with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool on 2026-09-14 UTC. They are not real observations, measurements, or evidence of a certified repair. They contain no photographed people. Copies served by the web app are identical fixture assets. These assets are provided with the project under MIT to the extent rights are held by the contributor.

Prompts: (1) original photo-like fictional suburban curb ramp with crumbled concrete beside yellow tactile paving, overcast, no people or real identifying location; (2) edit the same synthetic viewpoint to show an illustrative repaired concrete transition and restored tactile paving; (3) original synthetic broken-asphalt pothole near a curb, no people or identifying location. All prompts explicitly requested illustrative fictional evidence and no text or dimensions.

`docs/design/neighborhood-concept.png` was generated using the same built-in tool as an implementation concept. Prompt specified the requested warm off-white, dark ink, teal navigation/list/map application with the explicit local simulation banner. It is documentation, not shipped UI. No third-party stock photography is used.
